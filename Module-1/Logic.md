Module 1 – Logic / Working
Start the program.
Enter the employee name.
Enter the employee's basic salary.
Enter the total number of working days.
Enter the number of days the employee was present.
Enter the allowance amount.
Enter the deduction amount.
Calculate the attendance-based salary using:
(Basic Salary ÷ Total Working Days) × Days Present
Add the allowances to the attendance-based salary.
Subtract the deductions from the salary.
Calculate the net salary.
Display the employee's name, attendance salary, allowances, deductions, and net salary.
End the program.
